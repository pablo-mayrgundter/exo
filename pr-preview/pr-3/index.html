Exo Cafe

A bar on the NMI pad of a running 6502. The street outside is the chip's wiring, simulated transistor by transistor; the lights inside follow its buses, and the wall behind the bar is the Game of Life in its RAM.

Guests rez in voxels. Donate spare cycles to the jobs running on the bar's CPU (tap their chip tokens) and you sharpen up. Turn on your camera and your avatar wears your face and moves its arms with yours; the video never leaves your browser.
