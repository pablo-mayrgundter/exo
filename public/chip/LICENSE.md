# 6502.json

The MOS 6502 (revision D) layout polygons, transistor list and node names in
`6502.json` are converted, unchanged in content, from the visual6502 project's
`segdefs.js`, `transdefs.js` and `nodenames.js`
(https://github.com/trebonian/visual6502) by `scripts/import-visual6502.mjs`.
