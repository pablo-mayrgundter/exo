# Jukebox credits

Every track on the Exo Cafe jukebox is either a public-domain composition
(composer dead more than 70 years, and published before 1929) arranged
here for the four-voice APU, or an original written for Exo Cafe. The
arrangements and originals are under the project's MIT licence
([LICENSE](../../LICENSE)). No copyrighted melody, game theme or later
arrangement is transcribed or paraphrased: the Korobeiniki arrangement
takes the folk tune only, not the Game Boy Tetris arrangement's harmony
or bass, and the originals are new compositions, not sound-alikes.

- **Non-Maskable**: a 138 bpm trance anthem over Am–F–C–G, with a
  syncopated 3-3-2 stab hook, rolling triangle bass and a snare-roll
  breakdown. Named for the NMI pad the bar stands on.
- **Overclock Meadow**: a bouncy F-major overworld theme, with an octave
  bass and offbeat chord shimmer.

The song data is in [songs/](songs), one file per track, in the text format
documented at the top of [song.js](song.js).
